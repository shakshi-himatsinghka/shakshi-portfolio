a little backstory

How I started freelancing

Last year I was a marketing executive at a jewellery brand. A big part of my job was running influencer collaborations, so I was the point of contact for every creator we worked with.

For one store reel, I decided to edit it in-house instead of having the creator do it, because I had a specific direction in mind.

While we were coordinating, the creator asked me who had edited it. I said I had, and she really liked it. A while later she messaged me again, this time asking if I'd edit her own short-form videos.

That's how I landed my first freelance client. I wasn't looking for it; I was just doing my job, and the work found its way to the right person.

I've kept editing ever since. Today I work with Dipikka Poddaar as her talent manager and video editor, so I see a reel from both sides: what the brand needs from it, and what the creator needs it to feel like.

How it works

I build every reel in layers and check in with you after each one, so you see it come together and nothing has to be redone at the end.

  1. 1

    Send me the footage

    A Drive link is perfect. Add a reel you love the feel of and when you need it by.

  2. 2

    The cut

    I put the whole story together, every clip in order and every cut in place, and send it to you to approve.

  3. 3

    Text & overlays

    Once the cut is locked, I add the fonts, captions and overlays. You approve the look.

  4. 4

    Sound

    Last come the music and sound effects. One final okay from you and it's ready to post.
